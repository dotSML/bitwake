# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

NeoTorrent contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Participation should be safe regardless of experience, identity, background, appearance, disability, nationality, or personal beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when discussing code or decisions.
- Critique ideas and behavior rather than people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and moderation gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, impersonation, doxxing, and retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Contact the repository owner through an available private channel on their GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, open a detail-free issue asking for private maintainer contact; include no names, screenshots, or incident details in that issue. GitHub's own reporting and blocking tools remain available for platform-level abuse or immediate safety concerns.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a correction or warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban participation permanently according to the seriousness and pattern of the behavior. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is acting on behalf of NeoTorrent.
